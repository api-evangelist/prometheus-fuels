# Prometheus Fuels

> Prometheus Fuels is a Santa Cruz, California electrofuels company (founded 2019, Rob McGinnis, Y
> Combinator) that turns atmospheric CO2 into carbon-neutral methanol, gasoline, diesel, jet fuel and
> renewable natural gas using a "no-desorb" direct air capture system and its Faraday Reactor
> hydrocarbon electrolyzer, run on off-grid solar. It also markets an Ultra Long Duration Energy
> Storage (ULDES) product for data-center power. It is a physical manufacturer: as of 2026-08-05 it
> publishes NO product API, no developer portal, no SDK and no /llms.txt. The only machine-readable
> surface on its hosts is the WordPress REST API (wp/v2) that serves its own corporate website.
